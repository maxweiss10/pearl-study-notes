To identify patellofemoral pain, OA and meniscus tears
1
STANDING — inspection (varus, valgus or neutral)
2
SEATED — palpation of joint lines (palpating distal femur, proximal tibia) · examine for quad atrophy (patient straightens legs, compare side to side)
3
SUPINE — palpation of patellar facets · evaluate for effusion · range of motion: flexion, extension · McMurray test (meniscus)
Bonus maneuvers — supine: Lachman (ACL) · valgus stress (MCL) · varus stress (LCL) · posterior drawer (PCL)
4
STANDING — squat and Thessaly tests (meniscus)
