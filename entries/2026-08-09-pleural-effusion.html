Why the order matters
Diuretics concentrate pleural protein and LDH — ~25% of heart-failure effusions falsely meet Light's. Steps 3–4 re-test only that suspect group: sensitivity stays near 98%; specificity climbs from 74–83% into the low 90s.

ULN = upper limit of normal for serum LDH
